Return to the Throne

Created and written by Edward La Borde Jr. This action-adventure thriller connects African-diaspora history with contemporary struggles over heritage, human agency, and sovereignty.

Prologue v1 + Sequence 02, Part 1

This is an editorial working cut from Episode 1, not a completed or picture-locked film. The Prologue establishes the historical storytelling framework; Sequence 02 develops a rescue-and-escape operation in Ghana. Pacing, voices, sound, and visual finishing are being refined.

Open the working cut on Google Drive

Production approach

Story-first shot design, locked character and environment references, selected Runway-hosted generation models, controlled quality review, and editorial finishing in Adobe Premiere. The filmmaker supervises dramatic performance, pacing, visual continuity, and sound.

The series

A planned ten-episode serialized adventure in which African-diaspora stories and their modern echoes reveal questions of possession, identity, trafficking, and cultural heritage.
